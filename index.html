Features

Everything you need to juggle Roblox accounts

Built for players who main more than one account — alt grinders, testers, and families sharing a PC.

🗂️

Unlimited Account Slots

Store as many Roblox accounts as you want with custom nicknames, avatars, and notes. No cap, no cloud dependency.

⚡

One-Click Switching

Swap the active Roblox session in a single click. The app handles logout, cookie injection, and relaunch for you.

🔄

Auto Cookie Refresh

Background refresh keeps your saved sessions valid so you never get logged out mid-grind or after a long break.

🪟

Multi-Instance Launch

Run several Roblox clients at the same time, each pinned to a different account — perfect for trades and testing.

📦

Cookie Import / Export

Back up your account list as encrypted JSON, move it to a new PC, or import a batch of cookies in seconds.

🛡️

Local & Private

Everything is stored on your machine. No account credentials are sent to any server — open source and auditable.
